Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Linguistic Swan guide.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Readers rely on Linguistic Swan for clear, accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment in exchange for coverage, and we do not promote particular courses, publishers or products. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

Lessons draw on established linguistics textbooks, peer-reviewed research and recognised reference databases. Statistics such as speaker numbers are presented as estimates with appropriate caution.

Responsible writing

  • We describe language descriptively and treat all languages and dialects with respect.
  • We note where figures vary or where scholars disagree.
  • We explain technical terms in plain language with clear examples.
  • We avoid stereotypes about speakers of any language or dialect.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools are created by us and offer general guidance only.
