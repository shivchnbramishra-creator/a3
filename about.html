About Linguistic Swan

A free, independent learning space that makes the science of language clear, accurate and enjoyable for students, teachers and curious readers.

Our story

Why a swan?

A swan looks effortless on the surface while a great deal happens beneath the water. Language is the same: we speak without thinking, yet every sentence relies on intricate systems of sound, structure and meaning working out of sight.

Linguistic Swan was created to make those hidden systems visible. We write for first-year students, language learners, teachers and anyone who has ever wondered why words work the way they do.

Free to learn

All lessons and the glossary are free to read, with no sign-up required.

Accurate & balanced

We follow mainstream linguistic research and note where scholars disagree or figures vary.

Respect for every language

We treat all languages, dialects and sign languages as equally systematic and valuable.

How we work

From research to readable lessons

Our lessons draw on established textbooks, academic references and typological databases. Each lesson is reviewed for accuracy and clarity, and we update pages when better information becomes available.
